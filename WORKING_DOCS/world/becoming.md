# Becoming

This document defines becoming as a core world-level concept in NE3ULA.

It should be read alongside `philosophy.md`, the E3 human-engine docs, and the
Alchemist Pyramid material.

## Core Framing

A human being is not a fixed thing.

A human being is a living process shaped by perception, memory, meaning,
action, repetition, and response. We are always becoming something.

The question is not whether change is happening.
The question is whether that becoming is conscious.

## Repetition and Revision

Repetition is not destiny.

What has been formed can be examined.
What can be examined can be reinterpreted.
What can be reinterpreted can be reshaped.

Becoming is the process of participating consciously in your own formation.

## The Aim

The aim is not a better mask.
It is a more coherent self.

Authorship does not mean total control over life.
It means meaningful participation in how one responds to life.

The raw material of life - pain, desire, confusion, memory, fear, longing,
tension, contradiction - is not meaningless.
It is material.

The aim of becoming is not to construct a perfect self.
The aim is to participate more consciously in the shaping of one's life.
