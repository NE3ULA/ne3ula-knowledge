# NE3ULA E3 Editorial Design Kit — V1

## Direction
Restrained editorial minimalism: generous whitespace, precise hierarchy, thin orbital geometry, small alchemical signals, and infrequent dark pauses. The diagrams remain the most visually dense pages.

## Core palette
- Paper: #FBF9F5
- Ink: #17131F
- Night: #0A0812
- Warm white: #F6F1FF
- Gold: #D6A43B
- Violet: #9B5CFF
- Cyan: #25D9D0
- Muted lavender: #B7ADCA

Pink and lime are reserved for diagrams and exceptional semantic coding, not routine decoration.

## Usage rules
1. Use no more than one primary accent and one secondary accent on a standard prose page.
2. Keep ornaments outside the main reading column. Never place body text over orbital geometry.
3. Use the chapter-opening accent once per chapter.
4. Use the section divider only at major internal transitions—not between every subsection.
5. Pull-quote frames appear at most once per chapter.
6. Dark field-note pages are exceptional pauses: target 2–4 across the entire book, not one per chapter.
7. The E3 practice marker may introduce the chapter practice block.
8. Preserve ample whitespace; decoration should never be used to fill space mechanically.
9. SVG is the production master. PNG is the compatibility fallback.
10. Existing model diagrams remain unchanged unless separately approved.
