# NE3ULA Public Domain Model

This document defines a public-facing translation layer for describing NE3ULA.

It is not a replacement for the canonical repository ontology.
Its purpose is to make the ecosystem easier to explain on public surfaces
without collapsing WORLD, ENGINE, INTERFACE, DOMAINS, SYSTEMS, and OPERATIONS
into a misleading internal structure.

## Four-Domain Public Model

### 1. Music, Media, and Myth

NE3ULA creates music, visual identity, storytelling, symbols, and narrative
worlds that carry the emotional and cultural signal of the brand.

### 2. Technology and Interfaces

NE3ULA develops apps, AI systems, electronics, wearables, and interactive
experiences that translate philosophy and creativity into usable form.

### 3. Identity and Human Development

NE3ULA builds systems that help people understand themselves, navigate their
lives, and participate more consciously in their own becoming.

### 4. Community and Cultural Activation

NE3ULA creates educational, participatory, and social experiences that invite
people into the world and empower them to grow within it.

## Language Guidance

Avoid leading publicly with:

- identity engineer
- social empowerment operating system

Prefer:

- identity design
- human development
- self-authorship
- conscious becoming
- participatory worldbuilding
- cultural empowerment
- tools for transformation
